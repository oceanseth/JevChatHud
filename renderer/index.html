what the factual slider does:
Jev scores each message 0–100 on whether it asserts something checkable — fact vs opinion, not whether it's true. Messages scoring below the slider are hidden; it combines with the relevancy slider, and 0 means no constraint.
0pure opinion or reaction — hype, jokes, emote spam, questions
33speculation stated as fact — predictions, rumors, guesses
67the speaker's own experience — concrete, but only they could verify it
100a checkable claim about the world, the game, or the stream
